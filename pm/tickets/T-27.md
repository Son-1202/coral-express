## Bug 1 — missing-partner "ghost" reads as a glitch
The B4.6 hint (dotted outline of the missing partner) renders as a grey smudge at train-view scale, and it carries the same heart icon as a formed bond, so it reads as broken art.
Fix so it is unmistakably a *hint*:
- Draw the missing partner as a crisp **dashed outline** of its real silhouette (edge detect on the sprite alpha at the sprite's native pixel scale, 1 px dashes, light cream/white, ~60% opacity), no grey fill, gently pulsing; never blurred into a blob at any zoom / quality tier.
- Replace the heart with a small **"+" heart-in-bubble** (or a "?" bubble) in a different colour, so formed bonds (solid heart) and possible bonds look different.
- At most **one** ghost per carriage (the most valuable possible bond); keep ghosts out of the way (behind real fish, near the floor/edge).
- Tapping/hovering the ghost (or opening car detail) tells the player what it is, e.g. tooltip "Add a Watchman Goby to bond with Pistol Shrimp (+50%)".
- Same treatment in Tank View if ghosts appear there.

## Bug 2 — Tank View never shows shiny fish
`src/render/tank/assets.ts` `fishUrls(id)` always loads `/fish/<id>.png`; shiny fish must load `/fish/<id>_shiny.png` (and `_cut` fallback), with a procedural tint fallback only if missing. Shiny fish also get a subtle sparkle particle in Tank View, and the info card already shows "★ Shiny". Verify the train view uses shiny sprites too (fishMaterial/fishTexture).
