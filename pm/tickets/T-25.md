**Role:** DEV · **Effort:** high (fix ticket) · **Phase:** 7

## Diagnose first (write findings in your handoff)
Likely causes — verify each with measurements, not guesses:
1. Train world position read from the sim, which only changes every fixed 0.25 s tick → 4 Hz stepping while the camera eases every frame. Fix: render-side interpolation between the previous and current sim tick (`alpha = accumulator / tickDt`), or derive the visual train X from a continuous function of (leg progress + elapsed render time) that is exactly consistent with the sim at tick boundaries.
2. Camera follow/sway not frame-rate independent (e.g. `lerp(x, target, 0.1)` per frame) or fighting the train (overshoot/oscillation). Use critically damped smoothing with `1 - exp(-k·dt)`, follow the *interpolated* train position, keep sway subtle and slow.
3. Parallax layers / scenery scroll driven by sim position (stepping) or by dt with spikes; clamp dt (e.g. ≤ 1/20 s) for visuals; use the same interpolated position for every layer.
4. Wheel rotation / rail-joint rocking / steam must use the same smoothed speed.
5. Check pixel snapping: if sprites or the camera snap to whole pixels at different times, that also shimmers — snap consistently or not at all for the camera.
