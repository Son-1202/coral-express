## Goal
Full-screen Tank View per B4.15 as a module the controller (T-13) calls: `createTankView(ctx: { renderer, scene?, api: GameApi, assets }) → { enter(carId), exit(), update(dt), isActive() }`. Read `src/ui/types.ts` for `GameApi` (T-14) and `src/render/stage.ts` for renderer access; if they aren't there yet, define a minimal local interface and adapt at the end.

## Deliverables
1. Camera dolly into the selected carriage (smooth ~0.8 s ease) until the tank fills the screen; the coast keeps drifting behind the back glass (blurred); slosh on arrive/depart events; Esc / back button / pinch-out exits with the reverse dolly.
2. Tank scene: habitat backdrop (or selected backdrop variant) + sand color + layered plant sprites (`plants/<habitat>_<n>.png`) and placed decor (`decor/<id>.png`) in two depth bands; plant sway shader `offsetX = sin(time×freq + worldX×0.7) × amp × uv.y²` with per-plant freq/amp from data; plants bend away 0.5 s when a fish passes; water surface wobble line, caustics, bubble stream from an air stone, drifting particles.
3. Fish with the motion profiles from SPEC C (darter, hoverer, glider, sinuous, crawler, hopper, pulser, swarm (instanced 40–80), sessile) in `src/render/tank/motion/` — reusable by the train view later (export them). Depth: random Z, farther = smaller/bluer/softer. Mascot wears a tiny conductor's cap (procedural pixel cap sprite).
4. Watch mode: tap fish → camera follows; info card (name/nickname editable → `act` if sim supports nickname else local), species, level, bonds, output, shiny, stamp flavor line. Tap water → food pinch fish swim to (cosmetic).
5. Photo mode (P): hide UI, choose frame (`ui/photo_frames_{1,2,3}.png`, procedural fallback), save PNG (canvas `toBlob` download), "pin postcard" → stamp book (local storage list is fine).
6. Decorate mode (E / pencil): bottom tray of owned decor/plants (from sim inventory) → drag onto floor/back wall (snap to floor line), drag to move, wheel/pinch scale 0.8–1.2, tap flip, long-press remove; capacity meter; sand / backdrop / paint swatches → `act({type:'setCosmetic'})`; all placements persist via sim actions (`placeDecor`/`moveDecor`/`removeDecor`).
7. `/?debug=zoo`: grid of every species (all motion profiles), every habitat's plants swaying, and every Sight sprite animating — for QA motion review.
8. Screenshots `docs/qa/screens/T-15_*.png`: watch mode, decorate mode with tray, photo mode, zoo page; and a 3-frame sequence (0, 0.5, 1 s) of the zoo.

## Acceptance
Build + tests green; enter/exit smooth; no console errors; the zoo shows every profile moving (compare frames).
