# T-38 handoff — Festival Station decor + sights audit (DEV)

## Done
- **Floating sky bunting removed**, and so is the code-drawn pennant row on the train roof. That row is the "pennant row on the fence" in the producer screenshot.
- New `src/render/fx/festival.ts` (`FestivalDecor`):
  - The art hangs in 3 sagging spans at the station's depth, inside the sharp band: `festival/bunting` from the left flag pole to the left eave, `festival/lanterns` from eave to eave (flatter sag), then `festival/bunting` from the right eave to the sign's top-left corner, tucked behind the sign so the name stays clear.
  - UVs follow arc length, so tiles keep their aspect. The cord row is found from the alpha, and pennant tips sway with a vertex wave.
  - `festival/flag` poles stand at both platform ends with a rippling pennant.
  - Confetti uses `festival/confetti_1..8`, falling with spin, side drift and a new `flip` paper tumble. A light fall plays only while that platform is near the focus. On arrival there is a pop from the poles and eaves plus a 3.5 s shower. The old full-screen squares in `fxEvents.ts` are removed.
  - The decor **outlives the sight** (it ends on arrival) and is removed once the platform is 30 units from focus. It is not part of `clear()`, so a sight that starts on the arrival tick, or a second festival, leaves it in place (`fests[]`). Verified present at arrival and gone after departure, and present while a Whale Breach starts at arrival (`T-38_festival_l1_desktop_overlap_whale.png`).
  - Every texture has a procedural fallback (`festivalPaint.ts`).
- `station.ts`: per-Line `EAVES` fractions plus `festivalAnchors()`, which reads any of the pooled views. Spans re-hang when the layout changes.
- `assets.ts`: `PATHS.festival(name)`.
- **Sights audit**: the Stowaway painted "cone" fountain (code-drawn) is replaced by a particle splash fountain. Unused `buntingTex`/`fountainTex` are removed.
