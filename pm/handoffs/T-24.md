# T-24 handoff (DEV): final polish and the rail fix

## Status
Done. `npm run build` passes. `npm run test` passes: 27 files, 172 tests.

## Rails (top priority from the producer)
**Cause.** The train group sat at y 0.2. The side-on rail strip sat at z 0.56, 0.46 in front of the train, and was mostly buried in the track bed. The bed texture also had two painted rails. On screen, the wheels hovered over a grass strip with the rail drawn lower in front.

**Fix.**
- `src/render/diorama/config.ts`: new `RAIL_TOP` (0.2) and `RAIL_Z` (0.17).
- `src/render/diorama/diorama.ts`: the rail strip now stands on the bed, from y 0 to `RAIL_TOP`, at z 0.17, just in front of the train.
- `src/render/diorama/paintTrack.ts`: removed the painted rails from the bed. The sleepers and ballast stay.
- `src/render/stage.ts`: the train is now at y `RAIL_TOP - 0.08`. The art wheels end 0.05 above the group, so the rims tuck about 2 px behind the rail head.

**Checked.** I projected the points in the page: wheel bottom 469.7 px, rail top 470.4 px at 1280×720. Screenshots cover every Line, at stations and mid-leg, on desktop and phone. The platform face now runs down to the rail.

**Depot scene** (`src/render/depot/depotScene.ts`):
- Train y changed from 0.18 to 0.11, so the wheels sit on the rail head.
- The clear top rows of the rail texture rendered as a black line. They are now transparent (`alphaTest`).
- Before/after comparison: `T-24_rails_compare_depotscene_desktop.png`.

**Ending beach:** already correct, so no change (`T-24_rails_ending_beach_desktop.png`).

**Side fix** (`src/render/train/train.ts`): the coupling bar is now at buffer height. Before, it was a dark dash floating near the rails.
