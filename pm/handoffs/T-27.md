# T-27 handoff — readable missing-partner hint + shiny fish in Tank View (DEV)

**Status:** done. `npm run build` green, `npm run test` 193/193 (32 files, +10 new tests).

## Bug 1: missing-partner "ghost"
- **One ghost per carriage** (`src/render/fx/ghostPick.ts`, pure): bonds that are exactly one fish short are grouped by the missing species, so a pair and a community waiting for the same fish make one hint. The highest summed bonus wins (bonus values come from `bonds.json` and `config.communityBonus`). Communities that are two fish short get no ghost.
- **Look** (`src/render/train/fishMaterial.ts`, ghost branch): a crisp dashed inner-edge outline of the real sprite silhouette with no fill. The line is 1 texel wide, but never thinner than 1 CSS px (`fwidth` times the renderer pixel ratio, `uLinePx`). Dashes are cream (2 on, 1 off) with faint teal gaps. They run along the local edge axis and are anchored to the sprite, so they don't crawl. The opacity pulses between 0.45 and 0.75. Texture samples outside the quad count as transparent.
- **Placement** (`src/render/fx/bonds.ts`): the ghost sits in the bottom corner away from its partner, behind all fish (z −0.015), facing into the window. Its size is capped at 34% of the window width and 42% of its height.
- **Hint bubble** (`ghostHintTex` in `paintFx.ts`): a gold-rimmed cream bubble with a hollow heart and a teal "+" badge. It no longer uses the red formed-bond heart.
- **Tooltip** (`src/render/fx/ghostTip.ts`, a DOM element like SightBanner): appears on mouse hover, and for 3.2 s after a tap on phone. Example: "Add a Watchman Goby to bond with Pistol Shrimp (+100%) and complete the Mangrove Crew (+200%)". A tap also feeds the car, because the feed handler is in `src/game/input.ts`, which T-27 doesn't own. Car detail already lists possible bonds ("needs …"). A tap tooltip hides itself when Tank View opens.
- **Known limit:** the tooltip shows base bonus percentages from `bonds.json` and `communityBonus`. `BondFx` has no sim state, so it leaves out decor and upgrade percentage points, and the tooltip can show a lower bonus than the market estimate (`ui/helpers.ts`).
- `BondFx.ghostScreen()` returns the ghost's screen centre and tooltip text, for E2E.
- Tank View has no ghosts or hearts, so nothing was needed there.

## Bug 2: shiny in Tank View
- `src/render/tank/fishSource.ts` (pure, tested) picks the sprite in this order: `/fish/<id>_shiny.png`, then `/_cut/fish/<id>_shiny.png`, then the normal sprite with the procedural `shinyTint`.
- `actors.ts` caches textures per species plus shiny. `scene.ts` rebuilds a fish actor when its shiny flag changes.
- Sparkles: 4 twinkling 4-point stars per shiny fish (`src/render/tank/sparkles.ts` + `PointCloud` star mode in `shaders.ts`). They are committed and disposed like the other point clouds.
- The train view already used `_shiny` art (`fishView.fishTexture`). Verified in the screenshot.
