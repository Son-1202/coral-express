# T-40 handoff — DEV polish pass

**Status:** done. `npm run build` green, `npm run test` 41 files / 248 tests green (new: `tests/unit/game/bondPP.test.ts`, `tests/unit/ui/t40.test.ts`; `tank/layout.test.ts` updated for no-alpha depth). `scripts/motion-probe.ts` passes all 4 cases, unchanged from the baseline: cruise jitter 0.022 px, arrival→stop→departure 0.020 px.
T-39 art was already on disk when I started (05:36), so there was nothing to poll for.

## Items
1. **Dolphin Race** (`src/render/fx/seaStrip.ts` new, `fx/sights.ts`)
   - **Water strip:** a camera-relative strip of water slides up over the foreground band, taking 0.9 s to rise and 0.9 s to fall. Its waterline sits at NDC −0.64, so it clears the glass and the nav buttons on desktop and phone.
   - **Colours per Line:** sea on L1, slate fjord water on L2, jade lagoon on L3. The strip is shaded in world space with chunky pixels and is 160 u wide, so the camera's one-frame lag never shows an edge.
   - **Dolphins:** they sit just behind the opaque strip, so the water hides them below the waterline.
   - **Splashes:** droplets start at the waterline, just behind the plane, so they fall back into the water.
   - **Tap targets:** dolphins stay tappable through hit meshes. A real click on a dolphin ends the sight as a tap on desktop and on phone (verified).
   - **Dusk:** checked; the post grade tints the strip (`T-40_dolphins_l1_desktop_dusk.png`).
   - **Decision:** L2 uses the same strip in fjord colours. The fjord water in the far art is hidden by trees.
2. **Fish fade** (Tank View only; the train-view fish were already opaque). The z switch happens at `hidden` > 0.5. While its wearer hides, the mascot cap (drawn without depth test) is hidden too. I did not catch a hiding fish in a screenshot: Tank View exposes no debug handle, so this part is verified by code only.
   - `layout.depthLook` alpha is always 1. Depth now shows only as scale and blue tint.
   - `actors.ts` drops the hidden fade. A fish hiding in decor is drawn at z −4.5, behind all back-layer decor (−4) and in front of the ambient plants (−5).
3. **Tanks 20 % bigger**
   - **Scale:** `TRAIN_SCALE = 1.2` in `paintTrain.ts`. **Decision:** the loco scales with the carriages, so buffer and coupling heights stay matched.
   - `train.ts` scales `ART_PX`, `GAP`, the placeholder geometry and the link. `BODY_LIFT` is not scaled, so the wheels stay on the rail head.
   - **Fish and overlays:** `FISH_UNITS_PER_PX` is scaled, so fish grow with the tanks. Hearts, the hint bubble and the frenzy ring are scaled too.
   - **Camera:** with no car selected, `stage.followX` frames the loco plus the first 3 cars. This depends only on the train's composition, so the camera target doesn't switch at stops. A selected car is followed as before.
   - **Checked:**
     - Wheel bottom is 472.0 px and rail top 470.4 px at 1280×720 (`T-40_rails_l1_desktop_2x.png`).
     - Couplings meet.
     - Depot: cars roll to the buffer and the loco goes into the shed. The shed roof is visible on phone.
     - Ending beach is unchanged (its own 2D geometry).
   - **Stop position:** the middle of the framed span (loco + up to 3 cars) now stops 0.6 u behind the platform centre (`STOP_BACK`). It used to be the train middle − 2.2. With 3 cars, the loco's nose stops at the platform end (−0.05 u) and the rear car overhangs by 1.15 u. Before this fix the whole 2.8 u overhang was at the rear.
   - **Passengers:** they still walk at the cars after a live arrival (`T-40_arrival_passengers_l1_desktop.png`).
   - **Also:** removed the kelp interior override (`train/interior.ts`), so the new T-39 `interior_kelp` is used.
4. **Ending caption:** the plate is now the caption's own `.plate` class. It is set while the caption is hidden, and kept while it fades out. The class change waits for the fade (850 ms). Sampled in Playwright: 0 frames show a caption without its plate (dive→video, panorama→theEnd).
5. **Stowaway:** droplets get `vx += trainVel` (new `SightHost.trainVel`). Measured: droplet vx ≈ train speed. Headless runs at ~4 fps, where the render dt is clamped to 0.1 s; there the droplets still trail by position. At real frame rates they ride along with the car.
6. **Missing-partner hint**
   - `input.ts`: a tap on the ghost or its bubble (`BondFx.isGhostAt`) shows the tooltip and does not feed. Verified: frenzy is unchanged after the tap.
   - The tooltip % now includes decor and upgrade pp through the new shared `src/game/bondPP.ts`, which `ui/helpers.fishGainWithBonds` also uses. With Matchmaker: "+125%".
7. **Icons:**
   - Car detail Feed and Tank View buttons.
   - Today's Special free-slot and cosmetic medallions.
   - Sight toasts for festival, stowaway and sea fog (`ui/art.sightArt` falls back to `icons/sight_*`).
   - All of these fall back to the procedural versions when the art is missing.
8. **Zoo:** 5 columns, cells 10.6 u tall, labels that scale with the stage on a dark plate (ellipsis at cell width), fish ×2.2 (`FishActor` `sizeMul`, zoo only).
9. **GDD:** Depot Reset section now says "An eligible Depot Reset always grants ≥ 1 Ticket (D-032)".
