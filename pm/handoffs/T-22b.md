# T-22b handoff — ending polish, tank decor snap, offline postcard art (DEV)

## Status: done. One 1-line wiring change is needed outside my paths (see "Wiring").

## Fixes
- **Bug 5, ending beach** (`src/render/ending/beach.ts`, new `beachPaint.ts`, new `carriages.ts`, `owned.ts`)
  - The beach now shows the player's real carriages: the count matches (1 to 8), each one draws its painted `interior_<habitat>` crop, and its own fish swim inside using the T-15 movers. The air gap, water line and glass sheen sit under the real `car_shell.png`. The data comes from `endingCars(state.carriages, state.fish, species)`.
  - The black sand uses the L3 palette, with a dusk rim light, dunes, basalt pebbles and glints. The painted boulder row cropped from `scenery/l3_mid` is the foreground, clipped to the ground. The sea fills only between its surface and the sand, so it meets the beach at a point with a soft tint and an animated swash. The flat block and the hard edge are gone.
  - Steam is drawn by `PuffSteam`. It uses the same `paintPuff` sprite, rate, drift and fade as `render/train/steam.ts`.
- **Bug 6, panorama and credits** (`underwater.ts`, new `spread.ts`, `ending.css`, `ending.ts`)
  - The caption sits on a parchment plate with a brass edge in the upper third, for both the video and panorama beats. Swimmers stay below 30% of the screen height.
  - The cast is spread over a jittered grid on 3 depth layers (scale and alpha). Floor critters get evenly spaced anchors. Overlap is removed by an ellipse repulsion that runs 3 passes per frame.
  - The credits roll now starts right under the title and runs to `translateY(-100%)`, so the black gap is gone.
  - `seek()` now clears the start fade, which was leaving the preview black when it jumped straight to the dive.
- **Bug 11, decor snap** (`src/render/tank/layout.ts`, `decorate.ts`, `actors.ts`)
  - `snapPlacement(x, y, wall)` snaps all floor decor to the sand. Only `WALL_DECOR` (`wren_photo`) can hang on the wall.
  - `restingY()` also clamps decor at render time, so older saves that hold mid-water `y` no longer float.
- **Bug 11, offline popup**: the new module `src/ui/offline.ts` (+ `offline.css`) uses `ui/postcard_l<line>s<last station>` art, falls back to `ui/line_cover_<line>`, and then to the procedural sun. It adds a parchment "While you were away" plate and the station name. Dev page: `/src/ui/offline-preview.html?line=3&station=4`.
