## Decisions
- **Train direction:** the train always heads right and the locomotive stays in front. The world is an endless coast, and the odometer is `lifetime.arrivals + legProfile(t/leg)`. Signs show the real sim station names (current, next and previous). The view snaps on big jumps (offline, skipTo, reset).
- **Passengers** walk on the near platform edge in front of the train. If they walked behind it, the train would hide them.
- **Debug:** `skipTo` uses a scripted fast-forward. It grants Shells, builds the track and rides to the terminal. For depot resets it raises `runShells` to the minimum and resets onto the target line. The extra `__debug.spawnFish(species, carId?, {shiny, level})` places a fish directly, ignoring markets. Also available: `setSpeed(k)` and `stopBot()`. `runBot(name[, seconds])` lazy-loads `src/bots`.
