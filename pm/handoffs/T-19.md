# T-19 handoff — DEV: render fixes, living world, depot scene, loading + T-18 wiring

**Status:** done.
- `tsc` is clean in all T-19 paths.
- The only build blocker is `src/bots/greedy.ts(215)`, a syntax error in T-20's file that is being edited right now.
- 64 tests pass in game, tank, ui and audio. The new `tests/unit/tank/avoid.test.ts` adds 4 tests.

- No console errors in any capture.

## Done
1. **Stations:**
   - Station building is now opaque and alpha-tested, with `renderOrder -2`, so it always draws behind the train.
   - `STATION_FIT` in `diorama/station.ts` sets per-line px/unit (L1 72, L2 92, L3 76).
   - L3 crops the bottom 25% of its art through texture UV, which removes the painted rails.
   - Lamp glows follow the crop.
2. **Toasts** (`ui/toasts.ts`):
   - At most 3 visible; newer toasts replace older ones.
   - Postcards group into one card with "+N more postcards". Each tap opens the next one.
3. Deleted `render/placeholder.ts` and `render/train/motion.ts`.
4. **Living world** (`render/world/`):
   - Seeded roadside props on the verge (kept clear of station buildings).
   - Near foliage with a sway shader (`sway.ts`).
   - Wildlife (`wildlife.ts`): 3 species per Line, each with its own tween path. One spawns every 20–40 s.
   - Sky (`sky.ts`): gull flock boids, drifting clouds, L3 volcano smoke at the painted crater, L2 fog banks drifting with noise.
5. **Depot scene** (`render/depot/`):
   - About 7.3 s, skippable by tap, Esc, Space or the Skip button.
   - Shed in the Line's palette and the Line's far painting behind it.
   - The rear carriages uncouple one by one and roll onto the siding. Their fish hop into the depot pool and bob there. The loco leaves with the Heritage carriage.
   - The HUD is hidden during the scene, which then fades out.
   - Hook: `stage.onRunStart(line)`, plus a bus `runStart` event.
6. **Tank:** `tank/motion/avoid.ts` (`DecorSteer`):
   - Free swimmers bend around decor.
   - Every 15–30 s a swimmer may hide 3–5 s in coral_castle, ice_arch, sunken_rowboat or mangrove_root.
7. **Loading screen:**
   - A ticket card in `index.html` that shows before any JS runs.
   - `game/loading.ts` preloads only the current Line's art (around 15 files), then fades the card out. Other Lines load lazily.
8. **T-18 wiring** (`main.ts`):
   - `createAudio`, with wheel clack from `stage.trainSpeed`.
   - Depot theme while the Depot panel is open (polls the HUD state).
   - `title_loop` video behind the title.
   - Line splash on `lineOpened` and `onRunStart`, debounced so one batch shows only one splash.
   - Ending on the sim `ending` event: pause, then `runEnding`, then unpause and `exitEnding` in onDone.
   - The ending beach now uses the `l3_far_dusk` painting (small edit in `render/ending/beach.ts`).
9. **Debug:**
   - `skipTo('line2'|'line3')` now repeats runs to satisfy T-20's `opensAfterRuns`.
   - New `__debug.depotScene(cars)`.
   - `stage.depot.seek(s)`.
   - `stage.world.wildlife.spawn(camera, id)`.
