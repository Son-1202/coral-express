## Diagnosis

- **Terminus is bimodal (too fast or never) and needs only ~2 resets.** Tickets = floor(sqrt(runShells / 100000)) grows with every Line's ×25/×600 economy: greedy's first reset already pays ~1677 Tickets (median per reset), 22 at 5e7 run Shells and 3162 at 1e12, so all 29 Tickets of upgrades are bought after reset 1 and ticketMult reaches ×10³–×10⁴ after reset 2. Run 3 then starts on Line 3 with only the Mascot, but the cheapest Line 3 species costs 1.5e12 and track to l3s3 6.0e11 while carried-over income is ~1e8/s: a multi-hour wall. Games that reach the Terminus (~30 min) got a Stowaway (free Line 3 fish) before all 48 slots filled; the rest stall with a full train of level-50 Mascot-species fish. Fix in data: a gentler ticket curve (e.g. log or a smaller exponent / larger divisor per Line), cheaper first Line 3 species/track, or make each Line's first species affordable from the previous Line's end income.
- **Fares are ~1.5% of income (target 25–50%).** Fish output scales with level × milestones (up to 50 × 2³ = ×400) and ticketMult, appeal does not scale with level at all, so fare = appeal × 8 × stationMult × fareMult falls behind within minutes. Needs either a much larger `fareAppealFactor` / `stationMults`, or appeal that grows with level (a rules change).
- **First reset at ~12.8 min (target 15–20), p10 6 min.** Line 1 track to the terminal totals only 561600 Shells; greedy reaches l1s6 with run Shells ≫ 1.0e6, so the reset fires on arrival. Raising `lines[0].trackBase` (or minRunShells) ~1.3–1.5× moves it into range.
- **First pair at ~3.4 min (target ≤2.5).** Clownfish (500 Shells) is sold only at l1s3, so the pair needs track to station 3 (960) and a full shuttle leg there; cheaper l1s3 track or a cheaper Clownfish/anemone pair at stations 1–2 fixes it.
- **Goal gaps / dead time.** Between minutes 5–15 greedy saves for ×8 track steps and ×5 carriages; levels at ×1.15/level are the only filler, so typical gaps are 4–5 min and the longest no-purchase stretch is several minutes (much longer in stalled Line 3 runs). More mid-priced items per station (decor, slots) or a smaller `trackGrowth` would fill it.
- **Requests share is high and very variable (p90 61%).** Rewards are 180–300 s of current income, repeat every run (T-06-b) and most auto-complete (species/appeal); in stalled runs they are the main income. Shorter reward durations (e.g. 60–120 s) would bring the median into 5–15%.
- **Species coverage.** Terminal species (watchman_goby l1s6, snow_crab l2s6) are almost never bought: the reset rule fires on arrival at the terminal. pocket_whale is sold only at l3s6, the Grand Terminus itself, so it can never be bought before the ending (structural). Line 3 species follow the stall above.
- **Dominance.** The Mascot rule (highest-level fish) picks Pebble Damsel (cheap levels reach 50 first); it is the only catalogue species at the start of the next Line, so later runs fill all slots with it (p90 share ~80%). Run-1 resets are near the 35% line. A Mascot rule by output, or Mascot-species stacking limits, would fix the late runs.
- **Offline**: not measurable while the greedy median Terminus is censored (>120 min); the 8 h break mostly helps by pushing through the Line 3 wall (higher reach %).
- **Bonds matter little.** Pair +0.5/community +1 are small next to level and ticket multipliers; bond-never is close to greedy at min 15 and reaches the Terminus about as often (both limited by the Line 3 wall). Larger pair/community bonuses (or bonds that scale with level) would separate them.

## Definitions

- **Meaningful goal**: new species this run, carriage, extra slot, track, bond formed, milestone, request completed, Today's Special, terminal, line opened, Depot Reset becoming available, reset, upgrade. Gap = longest stretch between goals inside minutes 5–15 of each run (window edges count).
- **Dead time**: longest stretch (after minute 1) with no spending action (fish, level, carriage, slot, decor, habitat, track, special, upgrade); a stall at the end of a game that never reaches the Terminus counts.
- **Income at min 15**: average run Shells/s over the 60 s before minute 15 of run 1 (or before the first reset if it comes earlier).
- **Dominance**: share of the run's fish income (`state.speciesShells`) earned by the top species, sampled right before each reset.
- Fare / request shares are of all Shells earned in the game (fish + fares + sights + requests + offline).
